RA Field efforts completed by AIS-Murray that involve riding along with commercial fishers that regularly participate in the Invasive Carp Harvest Program (ICHP). Staff members serve as observers to ICHP participants as they collect information on their fishing efforts, by-catch of non-target species and actual harvest of Invasive Carp.
